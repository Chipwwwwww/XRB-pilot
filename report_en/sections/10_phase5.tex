% =====================================================================================================
\chapter{Phase 5 (v9--v10): a second instrument, and the pooled evidence}\label{ch:phase5}
% =====================================================================================================

\begin{glance}{v9--v10}
\textbf{Question.} Do the two timing claims of Phases 3--4 -- (1) inside the hard state, low-frequency variability adds
BH/NS information to colour; (2) at fixed colour, BHs have a lower characteristic frequency $\nu_c$ -- reappear on a
different instrument (NICER/XTI, 2017 onwards, 2--10\,keV), and how strong is the evidence when all usable sources of RXTE
and NICER are combined? \\
\textbf{Pre-declared primaries.} v9a: NICER hard-like observation AUC, H$_N$+T$_N$-LR $-$ H$_N$-LR; v9b: colour-conditional
source permutation test of $\log_{10}\nu_c$ in NICER hard-like observations; v10a: equal-weight mean of the hard-state
timing gains of three subsamples (RXTE v2, RXTE external, NICER full observations); v10b: pooled colour-conditional
$\log_{10}\nu_c$ difference over both instruments with a joint permutation. \\
\textbf{Data.} NICER: 368 observations of 63 sources (9 BH, 54 NS), first $\approx6$ segments per observation (14.2\,GB) in v9,
full observations (further 24.2\,GB) in v10; the RXTE samples of Phases 3--4. Union of sources: 86 (16 dynamically
confirmed BHs). \\
\textbf{Verdict.} NICER reproduces both effects in direction and size (hard-like AUC 0.68 $\to$ 0.80; $\Delta\log_{10}\nu_c=-0.31$),
but both v9 primaries are \textbf{not detected} by a small margin (v9a $+0.112$ [$-0.004$, $+0.229$], v9b $p=0.136$). Pooled,
both v10 primaries are \textbf{detected}: $+0.145$ [$+0.028$, $+0.257$] and $D=-0.368$\,dex, $p=0.0012$, robust to leaving out
any single BH. \textbf{v10 is the overall strength of the evidence, not an independent confirmation}: the hypotheses and the
pooling were specified after v5--v9, and two of the three subsamples had been seen.
\end{glance}

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Motivation}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the end of Phase 4 the hard-state timing gain was detected on the RXTE v2 sources but not on external RXTE sources, and
the RXTE archive offered no further confirmed BHs. NICER is independent in instrument, band ($\approx0.5$--12\,keV; 2--10\,keV
used here), epoch (2017 onwards) and source mix; it covers two dynamically confirmed BHs never used before in this project
(MAXI J1820+070, Swift J1727.8$-$1613) with many hard-state observations, and its time resolution reaches the full 0.1--10\,Hz
band of \citet{RemillardMcClintock2006} and beyond. The RXTE models are not transferred (bands and responses differ): the same
pre-declared design is repeated on NICER. v10 then asks the question that the single-instrument tests could not settle
because each was at the edge of significance: what do all usable sources say together?

\subsection{NICER sources and observations (v9)}
\textbf{Sources.} BH: the 25 dynamically confirmed systems of \citet[Table 1]{Marcel2026}; NS: the 115 burster entries of the
MINBAR source table, minus 9 that have another entry within $0.05^\circ$ (unresolvable in the NICER field of view, radius
$\approx0.05^\circ$). Observations were found in the \code{nicermastr} table (target within $0.05^\circ$, exposure $\ge1$\,ks);
sources with $\ge5$ such observations: 11 BH and 55 NS (\file{results/v9/attrition\_catalog.csv}).

\textbf{Sampling.} Each source's observations were split into 8 time bins; within a bin candidates were tried in a random order
(\code{default\_rng(SEED + position)}) up to 3 times: 522 bins, 792 tries, \textbf{368 accepted} (BH 9 sources, NS 54). Rejections:
2--10\,keV rate $<20$\,c\,s$^{-1}$ (239) and fewer than 3 valid segments (185) (\file{data/v9/observations.csv}). SS 433 and
GS 2023+338 (V404 Cyg) had no qualifying observation.

\textbf{Download.} Cleaned event files (\code{ni<obsid>\_0mpu7\_cl.evt.gz}) were read as a stream through the HEASARC AWS mirror
(byte-identical, $\approx3.5$\,MB\,s$^{-1}$ instead of 0.1--0.4\,MB\,s$^{-1}$), with 16\,MB HTTP range requests, stopping after six
gap-free 128-s segments, at the end of the file or at 300\,MB (14.2\,GB in total, stored outside the repository).

\textbf{Features} (\cref{sec:nicer}; all pre-declared): 2--10\,keV events (PI 200--999), 128-s segments of $1/256$-s bins
($N=32768$, Nyquist 128\,Hz) cut from the good intervals (gap $>1$\,s ends an interval), burst segments removed with the v4 rules
on the 1-s 2--10\,keV light curve ([start $-20$\,s, end $+200$\,s]); an MPU is on if all eight 16-s blocks have counts; $\ge2$ MPUs.
All-pairs cross-MPU cospectra, ratio of means and signed square root (the v8 lesson); no background subtraction (rate cut
20\,c\,s$^{-1}$). Colours $H_N=[c_1,c_2]$ with $c_1=C(4\text{--}6)/C(2\text{--}4)$ and $c_2=C(6\text{--}10)/C(4\text{--}6)$; timing
$T_N$ = fractional rms in the v5 bands (0.016--0.094, 0.10--1.0, 1.0--4.0\,Hz); state from the 0.1--10\,Hz rms with the RM06
thresholds; $\nu_c$ from 13 octave bins over $1/128$--64\,Hz (\cref{eq:nuc}).
% CHECK: reports/preregistration_v9.md says nu_c uses the v6 definition, but scripts/v9lib.py weights the 13 octave bins by
% max(P_b,0) without dividing by Delta ln nu (v6 divides). For octave bins Delta ln nu is ~ln 2 for all but the lowest bins
% (j in [1,1] gives ln 3), so the two definitions differ only through the lowest bins. Code wins: the report states the code.

\textbf{Gates.} IC1: the streaming parser equals \texttt{astropy} on complete and truncated synthetic files; IC2: synthetic events
recover sinusoids at 0.05, 0.5 and 2\,Hz as 0.099--0.102 (true 0.1), the 0.1--10\,Hz rms as 0.143 (true 0.141), pure Poisson
$|z|\le1.3$, and Lorentzians with $\nu_0=0.5$ and 5\,Hz give $\nu_c=0.55$ and 4.18\,Hz; IC3: Cyg X-2 and GX 17+2 are soft-like in
15/16 observations (0.94). All passed.

\textbf{States.} \Cref{tab:p5states} gives the NICER states. The hard-like stratum has 31 BH observations of 7 sources (MAXI
J1820+070 7, Cyg X-1 7, Swift J1727.8$-$1613 5, GX 339$-$4 4, GRS 1915+105 4, 4U 1543$-$47 3, LMC X-3 1) and 111 NS observations of
32 sources.

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Models and statistics in this phase}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Models are the fixed LR and RF (\S\ref{sec:lr}, \S\ref{sec:rf}); there is no selection. The new statistics are the pooled bootstrap
and the pooled permutation test (\cref{sec:bootstrap,sec:permutation}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Pooled gain} (v10a). For a bootstrap draw $b$ of the source union (BH and NS resampled separately, sorted names,
\code{default\_rng(42)}), each component $m$ computes the hard-like AUC difference on the observations of its drawn sources
(with multiplicity), $\Delta_m^{(b)}$; the pooled value is $\bar\Delta^{(b)}=\frac13\sum_m\Delta_m^{(b)}$ (draws where a component is
undefined are dropped). The interval is the 2.5--97.5\% percentile range.
\item \textbf{Pooled $\nu_c$ difference} (v10b). For instrument $i$, $D_i$ = mean residual of BH sources minus mean residual of NS
sources (label-free kNN residualisation on the instrument's colours, leave-one-source-out); $D=\frac12(D_\mathrm{RXTE}+D_\mathrm{NICER})$.
The null permutes labels within the three membership strata (RXTE only, NICER only, both) with the number of BHs per stratum
fixed and the same label on both instruments for shared sources; 20,000 permutations, seed 42, $p=(1+\#\{|D_\pi|\ge|D|\})/(1+20000)$.
Synthetic calibration: null rejection rate 0.065 (200 simulations), power 0.98 for a BH shift of $-1.5$\,SD (50 simulations).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{NICER alone: hard-state timing (v9a)}\label{sec:p5v9a}

\section{Interpretation}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\textbf{What the data support directly.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item On an independent instrument and epoch the colour-only hard-like AUC is 0.68 (RXTE 0.65) and the soft/hard gap reappears;
timing raises the hard-like AUC by $+0.11$ (LR) to $+0.24$ (RF), but the pre-declared LR interval touches 0.
\item At fixed colour the NICER BHs have $\nu_c$ lower by $\approx2$ (same size as on RXTE), with $p=0.136$ in v9 and $p=0.033$ with full
observations.
\item Pooling all usable sources of both instruments (86, 16 BH), both effects are detected -- hard-state gain $+0.145$ [$+0.028$,
$+0.257$], $\Delta\log_{10}\nu_c=-0.37$\,dex, $p=0.0012$ -- and neither depends on any single BH.
\item RF gains more than LR, so timing and colour interact non-linearly.
\item NICER timing features are reliable (split-half 0.8--0.9).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What the data do not support.} The pooled results are not an independent confirmation: both hypotheses and the pooling rule
were written after v5--v9, and the RXTE v2, RXTE external and NICER prefix results were known; only the NICER full-observation
measurement was new (on the same observations). The 16 BHs are the only dynamically confirmed BHs in the analyses.

\textbf{Possible explanations (not verified).} A lower characteristic frequency fits the picture of time scales growing with the
compact-object mass or inner disc radius (BHs $\approx5$--20\,$M_\odot$, NSs $\approx1.4\,M_\odot$), but the factor $\approx2.3$ is smaller than the
mass ratio and may also reflect accretion rate/luminosity, which is not controlled (no Eddington ratios). On NICER the information sits
in 0.1--4\,Hz (T2, T3), on RXTE in 0.016--1\,Hz (T1, T2), plausibly because NICER prefixes are short (T1 noisy) and its band softer.
GRS 1915+105's obscured NICER-era state is the main BH error. Some NS ``hard-like'' NICER observations may be dips, eclipses or
background-dominated. The external RXTE component is smallest, perhaps because the external NSs are mostly AMXPs with strong
low-frequency noise (IGR J00291+5934).

\textbf{Hypotheses that need more data.} A genuinely independent, prospective test with frozen features and rules on sources and
instruments never used (Insight-HXMT, AstroSat, future NICER transients) -- this is Phase 6; control of the Eddington ratio; Lorentzian
fits instead of the $\nu P_\nu$ centroid; a usable rule such as ``hard-like and $\nu_c<1$\,Hz $\Rightarrow$ BH-like''.

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Deviations and caveats}\label{sec:p5caveats}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Written after earlier results, self-approved}; v10 explicitly not an independent confirmation.
\item \textbf{v9 download} (before any model): HEASARC AWS mirror instead of the main site (byte-identical), curl range requests;
raw files outside the repository (14\,GB).
\item \textbf{v9 post hoc}: colour-window $\nu_c$ comparison, the source-level v9c version and the $c_2>0.7$ exclusion were decided after the
results.
\item \textbf{v10 permutation} (before any v10b statistic): union-wide permutation replaced by permutation within instrument-membership
strata, motivated by a synthetic power test; a bug with single-instrument strata (object array of equal-length tuples) was fixed without
changing the statistics; scatter jitter seeded by \code{crc32}.
\item \textbf{v10 resources}: feature parallelism reduced from 4 to 2 workers (OneDrive used $\approx10$\,GB RAM); results unaffected.
\item \textbf{Sample sizes}: 7 BH sources in each instrument's hard-like stratum (GRS 1915+105 anomalous, LMC X-3 one observation); only
two BHs had never been used before.
\item \textbf{Measurement}: NICER prefixes in v9 (T1 noisy); no NICER background subtraction (only a 20\,c\,s$^{-1}$ cut and a background
sensitivity analysis); 12 bright observations truncated at 1\,GB; RXTE and NICER bands and $\nu_c$ ranges differ, so their $D$ values are
not strictly comparable; the two $\nu_c$ definitions differ in their bin weights (see the CHECK in the data section).
\item \textbf{Bootstrap and permutation} keep the predictions and features fixed.
\end{itemize}

% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Takeaway}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICER repeats both timing effects with the same size as RXTE, each just short of the pre-declared threshold on its own; pooled over
86 sources and 16 dynamically confirmed BHs, both are detected and robust to dropping any BH. Because the hypotheses and the pooling
were chosen after the component results, Phase 5 measures the overall strength of the evidence rather than confirming it. Phase 6
therefore freezes a rule and tests it prospectively on new observations.
