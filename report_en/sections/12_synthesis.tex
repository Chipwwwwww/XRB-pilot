\chapter{Synthesis across phases (v14)}\label{ch:synthesis}
% =====================================================================================================
Version v14 (2026-10-06) ran no new test. It collected the results of v1--v13 into a final summary, two tables and one figure, and
decided to stop the series because any further version could only re-use the same sources. The files are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \file{reports/final\_summary\_zh-TW.md} (summary text);
\item \file{results/final\_timing\_gain\_across\_versions.csv} and \file{results/final\_nuc\_difference\_across\_versions.csv} (tables);
\item \file{figures/final/summary\_across\_versions.png}, made by \file{scripts/64\_final\_summary\_figure.py}.
\end{itemize} This chapter puts all pre-declared primary comparisons side by side,
shows the two timing results across samples and instruments, and states the conclusions in three tiers together with the degree of
independence behind each.

\section{Conclusions in three tiers}\label{sec:synthtiers}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{keybox}{Supported by the data}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Two count-space hardness ratios capture essentially all the BH/NS information in a single 5--25\,keV RXTE spectrum: neither the full
shape, nor intensity, nor 3--5\,keV, nor 25--60\,keV, nor flexible algorithms, nor 17$\times$ more observations add anything detectable
(Phases 1--2).
\item The colour information works in soft states and fails in hard states: observation-level AUC 0.954 (soft-like) vs 0.646 (hard-like) on
RXTE, difference $+0.31$ [$+0.10$, $+0.51$]; colour-only hard-state AUC 0.50--0.75 on NICER (Phase 4--6).
\item Inside the hard state, 0.016--4\,Hz variability raises the BH/NS separation, consistently on RXTE, on three disjoint NICER observation
sets, in the cross-instrument pool, after background correction, and prospectively with a frozen pipeline and a pre-chosen RF on new
observations (v13: $+0.18$ [$+0.05$, $+0.34$]).
\item Type-I burst contamination does not drive any of the spectral conclusions (v4, v7b1).
\end{enumerate}
\end{keybox}

\begin{keybox}{Consistent in direction, marginal in significance}
\begin{enumerate}
\item At the same colour, the characteristic frequency of BH variability is lower than that of NSs by 0.3--0.6\,dex in every data set except
the four-BH external RXTE set; pooled $p=0.0012$ (v10b), but single NICER data sets give $p$ between 0.02 and 0.21. No Eddington-ratio
control was possible, so mass and accretion state cannot be separated.
\item The timing gain is stable for the non-linear RF ($+0.17$ to $+0.25$) and unstable for LR ($+0.07$ to $+0.26$).
\item On MAXI, the 2--4\,keV colour carries BH/NS information beyond the $\ge4$\,keV colour that is not explained by Galactic absorption
(v8d detected; v9c post hoc in all C4 tertiles).
\end{enumerate}
\end{keybox}

\begin{cautionbox}{Not supported}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A broad-band 512--1024\,Hz criterion in the style of \citet{SunyaevRevnivtsev2000}: after removing a cross-PCU white component it is
essentially undetectable at single-observation sensitivity (v8c, exploratory).
\item High accuracy of linear colour models on MAXI with confirmed BHs: LR has source AUC 0.45--0.59 (v7c2); the high accuracy of
\citet{deBeurs2022} comes mostly from the two NS classes.
\item More flexible models, nested model selection, wider bands or more observations per source as a route beyond two colours (v4).
\end{enumerate}
\end{cautionbox}

\section{How independent is each piece of evidence?}\label{sec:synthladder}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
\Cref{tab:synthladder} places the timing results on an ``independence ladder''. Every pre-registration was written after the results of all
earlier versions, so later hypotheses were increasingly guided by earlier data; the project compensated by freezing pipelines and rules
before new data (v6, v8b, v11, v13). No step reaches source-level independence: the BH side always consists of the same 16 dynamically
confirmed BHs (7--9 in the hard state per instrument).

\section{Why the series stopped}\label{sec:synthstop}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The decision log records the v14 decision to stop after v13: 16 of the 25 dynamically confirmed BHs of \citet{Marcel2026} had been used,
the remaining ones have no usable RXTE or NICER timing data, and batch access to other missions could not be verified. A further version
could only add new observations of the same sources, which would not change the level of independence. The natural next step is a
source-level prospective test on new BH transients (\cref{ch:future}).
