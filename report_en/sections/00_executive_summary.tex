% =====================================================================================================
\textbf{Question.} Can one X-ray observation classify the compact object (BH or NS) of a source that was never used for training?
\textbf{Design.} Dynamically confirmed BHs only; leave-one-source-out evaluation; source-level balanced accuracy and AUC with a
2000-draw class-stratified source bootstrap; a pre-registered primary comparison per sub-analysis, ``detected'' only if its 95\% interval
excludes 0 (or $p<0.05$ for permutation tests); frozen models and rules for every test on new data. \textbf{Each pre-registration was written
after the results of all earlier versions}; from v5 on they were designed and approved by the analyst under the user's standing
instruction.

\begin{keybox}{Supported by the data}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Two count-space hardness ratios capture essentially all BH/NS information in a 5--25\,keV RXTE spectrum: source BA 0.80, source AUC 0.92
for the two-colour logistic regression on 31 sources; nothing tested beyond them (full shape, intensity, 3--5 and 25--60\,keV, eight
algorithms with nested selection, all 7,949 observations, 15 more sources) improves the ranking of unseen sources (\cref{ch:phase1,ch:phase2}).
\item The colours work in soft states and fail in hard states: AUC 0.954 vs 0.646, difference $+0.309$ [$+0.099$, $+0.510$] (\cref{ch:phase4}).
\item Inside the hard state, 0.016--4\,Hz variability adds information: $+0.264$ [$+0.048$, $+0.455$] on the RXTE sample; $+0.145$ [$+0.028$, $+0.257$]
pooled over RXTE and NICER; $+0.184$ [$+0.049$, $+0.337$] prospectively on new NICER observations with a frozen pipeline and a pre-chosen
random forest (\cref{ch:phase4,ch:phase5,ch:phase6}).
\item Type-I bursts do not affect the spectral conclusions (v4 detector, MINBAR catalogue).
\end{enumerate}
\end{keybox}

\begin{keybox}{Consistent in direction, marginal in significance}
\begin{enumerate}
\item At fixed colour, BH variability has a lower characteristic frequency, by 0.3--0.6\,dex in every data set but the four-BH external RXTE
set; pooled $p=0.0012$, but $p=0.07$--0.21 in the background-corrected and prospective NICER tests; no Eddington-ratio control.
\item The timing gain is stable for random forests ($+0.17$ to $+0.25$) and unstable for logistic regression ($+0.07$ to $+0.26$).
\item On MAXI/GSC the 2--4\,keV colour carries information beyond the $\ge4$\,keV colour that survives a Galactic $N_\mathrm{H}$ correction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{keybox}

\begin{cautionbox}{Not supported, and the main caveat}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Flexible models do not beat two colours; nested automatic selection is worse (inner AUC 0.96--1.00, outer 0.75).
\item A broad 512--1024\,Hz ``NS noise'' criterion is not detectable at single-observation sensitivity (exploratory).
\item Linear colour models on MAXI with confirmed BHs have almost no discriminating power; a published high accuracy reproduces exactly but rests
mainly on the NS classes.
\item \textbf{All confirmations are at the observation level on the same 16 dynamically confirmed BHs}; v10 pooling is overall evidence, not
an independent confirmation; v11--v13 are new observations of the same sources, not new sources; RF was chosen post hoc in v12 and
pre-specified in v13.
\end{enumerate}
\end{cautionbox}
